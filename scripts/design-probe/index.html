2 · Blending (item 7)

  1. Stand about a metre from a plain pale wall, in even light. Start AR: black patches at 25%, 50% and 75% appear 60 cm ahead, with wall showing between them.
  2. Take a screenshot and check the wall shows beside each patch in it (Quest: hold the Meta button and press the trigger; Android: power and volume down). Send it to the Mac.
  3. In the headset, match by eye too: tap the grey swatch that matches the bare wall, then the one that matches the 50% patch.
  4. Last, tap the lowest AR chip whose text reads cleanly.

iPhone (AR Quick Look): open , point it at a pale wall so the patches stick to it, and take a screenshot with wall beside each patch.

3 · Flicker (item 8)

Five flicker variants, labelled 1 to 5 in a random order that changes every time. Watch each for a full minute, then rate it.

On a real table (AR): on Quest this needs Space Setup with the table marked. Pick the table’s colour on the panel, aim at the tabletop until the ring sits on it, then pinch or pull the trigger to place the hologram.

In the room (VR): six holograms around you, all flickering at once.

4 · Sparks frame time (item 9)

About 90 seconds. First the page measures the display’s own refresh rate, then it alternates “sparks on the focused hologram only” with “sparks on every hologram” (15 s each, twice), then doubles the sparks until frames start running long. Keep still and do not touch the screen until the result appears.

Phone: nine cards, each its own canvas at up to 1.5× pixel density, like the feed. With focus only, eight cards hold still, as the feed does; with sparks everywhere, all nine draw every frame.

Quest: six holograms around you with a table light each. If you have Meta’s OVR Metrics Tool, turn on its overlay first and screenshot the GPU figures during each phase: the page can only see dropped frames, not how close the GPU is to its limit.

5 · Poke targets and type size (item 10)

Quest with hand tracking on (put the controllers down). A grid 45 cm away lights one square at a time; poke it with your index finger. 120 pokes, with three square sizes mixed at random. Then type samples appear one scale at a time, at 45 cm and at 1.75 m; rate each.
