# Embodied Communication

## Hypothesis

Presence becomes useful when the recipient can read and act on cues that are
lost in text or flat video: body orientation, timing, gaze, gesture, scale,
voice prosody, and relation to nearby objects or people.

## Seeds in Scope

- IDEA-011 Co-presence Portal
- IDEA-012 Hybrid embodiment
- IDEA-024 Presence authenticity watermark
- IDEA-028 Embodied rhetoric controls

## Product Directions

- Capture face, gaze, hands, body, and voice as coordinated expressive layers.
- Preserve human scale and spatial audio in live VR, AR, and asynchronous playback.
- Let creators deliberately amplify meaning with expression markers instead of
  relying only on inferred emotion.
- Make provenance visible when an appearance or performance has been materially edited.

## Design Tension

Higher realism can improve social presence, but it can also trigger discomfort,
privacy concerns, and uncertainty about authenticity. Fidelity must be chosen
for communication benefit, not as an end in itself.

## Open Questions

- Which cues most improve comprehension for remote instruction?
- What is the acceptable boundary between expressive editing and deceptive editing?
- How should consent, recording scope, and replay rights work?
