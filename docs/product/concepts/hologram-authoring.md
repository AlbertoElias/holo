# Hologram Authoring

## Hypothesis

Creators need to shape a message before and after capture. The authoring model
should preserve the spontaneity of embodied performance while allowing repair,
clarity, and composition.

## Seeds in Scope

- IDEA-020 Multi-take ghost overlay
- IDEA-021 Segment retakes
- IDEA-022 Message-first editor
- IDEA-023 Clarity co-director
- IDEA-028 Embodied rhetoric controls

## Authoring Layers

- Performance: body, face, hands, and gaze.
- Voice: live voice, narration, emphasis, and silence.
- Environment: scene bubble, physical context, and props.
- Spatial language: symbols, diagrams, anchors, and links.
- Presentation: camera, pacing, recap, and viewer checkpoints.

## Product Directions

- Retake only a selected segment and blend it into the surrounding performance.
- Make body, voice, objects, and annotations independently editable.
- Let creators convert a gesture into a clean editable spatial primitive.
- Offer clarity suggestions such as replacing "this" with an anchored reference.
- Show ghosted prior takes to support repeatable demonstrations and choreography.

## Design Tension

The editor must be more expressive than a timeline editor but less demanding
than a full 3D animation suite. The intended user is communicating, not
becoming a technical artist.

## Open Questions

- Which edits are valuable enough to justify complex interpolation tools?
- Where should AI make a suggestion versus automatically modify a recording?
- What is the fastest way to add text, images, video, and charts to a hologram?
