# Product Portfolio Directions

**Layers:** All layers (portfolio).

**Status:** Alternative directions, none selected by this document.

These directions preserve the earlier productization discussion. They are
possible audiences and business models, not a list of features to implement.
The medium's [three promises](../VISION.md) can be evaluated within any of them.

| Idea | Direction | Communication problem to investigate | Extra assumptions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IDEA-064 | Consumer creator-to-feed network | Embodied expression, storytelling, connection, and sharing demonstrations | Repeat creation, recipient demand, growth loops, discovery, and moderation |
| IDEA-065 | Enterprise training/review | Explain procedures, review designs, annotate sessions, and preserve team context | Team adoption, private libraries, identity, workspaces, and access controls |
| IDEA-066 | Commerce AR | Explain a product's scale, use, placement, or behavior before purchase | Merchants can make useful content and recipients benefit beyond a bare 3D model |
| IDEA-067 | API/SDK platform | Let other applications create, distribute, and render spatial messages | A concrete external integrator needs stable contracts and hosted capabilities |

Rapid creator iteration, AR/VR team demos, cross-device playback, and
deterministic XR regression testing were proposed early uses of the pipeline.
The testing capability helps development; it does not prove recipient value.

The [historical proposal](../research/historical-productization-plan.md)
recommended consumer creator-to-feed first because it matched the architecture,
with teams later. Subsequent discussion broadened the focus to communication
and the later assistant recommended spatial understanding first. Preserve both
as recommendations, not an implied market decision.

Use [APPROACH.md](../APPROACH.md) to choose a reachable speaker, recipient,
and repeated task. Decide on a market after learning what users return for,
rather than assuming a feed UI determines the product's destination.
