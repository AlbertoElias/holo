# Communication Protocols

**Layers:** meaning, conversation.

## Hypothesis

Holograms become a communication medium when they help people establish shared
understanding, repair misunderstandings, record decisions, and carry work
forward. Visual richness alone is insufficient. Protocols live in the meaning
layer and are anchored to the event, so a decision or question keeps pointing
at what it was about.

## HoloGrammar

HoloGrammar is the schema of the meaning layer:

```txt
Utterance = Intent + Referent + SpatialAction + TimeScope + Confidence + ResponseContract + Provenance
```

- **Referent** is always in the event: a bone, an object, a place, or a moment.
- **Provenance** is recorded, inferred, or added.

An utterance might be: "This wrist rotation is a risk at 00:12; inspect it and
approve or request a change." The risk is attached to the wrist and appears
only for the relevant interval. Its referent may come straight from the body:
the author pointed at their wrist while saying "this".

## Core Protocols

- Grounding loop: present, acknowledge, paraphrase if needed, then lock shared understanding.
- Reference lock: identify the person, object, region, or moment under
  discussion, read from pointing and gaze when possible, and invite others'
  views to it without taking control.
- Divergence: fork alternatives, compare them in the same scene, then merge or preserve the disagreement.
- Decision: create a durable decision stamp containing rationale, alternatives, and owner.
- Repair: create a question beacon, clarify in context, and resolve the thread.
- Handoff: convert a decision into task tokens that retain the scene context.
