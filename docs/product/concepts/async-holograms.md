# Async Holograms

## Hypothesis

An asynchronous hologram can preserve the context of a live explanation while
letting the viewer explore, respond, and revisit specific moments. It should
not be a passive recording.

## Seeds in Scope

- IDEA-002 Contextual annotations
- IDEA-016 Branching holograms
- IDEA-017 Interactive checkpoints
- IDEA-018 Holo Replies
- IDEA-019 Semantic diff
- IDEA-025 Meaning heatmap
- IDEA-029 Conversational memory palace

## Product Directions

- Bind notes, questions, and replies to objects, regions, motion states, and time.
- Let viewers choose a branch, inspect an object, or answer a checkpoint.
- Let recipients answer in the same spatial medium or through a capable desktop fallback.
- Represent versions as meaningful changes to speech, motion, artifacts, and intent.
- Surface aggregate comprehension signals while protecting viewer privacy.

## Example

A field expert records a repair walkthrough. At the critical motion, a viewer
can freeze the scene, inspect the relevant part, ask a question attached to the
part and moment, and record a reply that the expert can address in context.

## Open Questions

- Which kinds of interaction preserve the creator's narrative while empowering viewers?
- How should branches and replies be presented without becoming a maze?
- What is the minimal desktop experience that still feels holographic?
