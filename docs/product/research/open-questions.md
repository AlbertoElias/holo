# Product Research Questions

These questions should be investigated before they become implementation
requirements. They are supporting notes for ideas and PRDs, not a lifecycle
state, and are intentionally not decisions; see `../README.md`. They are
grouped by the layers in [what a hologram is](../concepts/what-a-hologram-is.md).

## The medium

1. Which communication tasks show a measurable comprehension or handoff benefit
   from embodied, spatial explanation compared with video and documents?
2. On a phone, does a portal (moving the phone moves the camera) measurably
   beat a fixed-camera clip of the same hologram for finding what the author
   meant?

## Event

3. Which avatar fidelity signals improve trust and comprehension, and which
   increase uncanny-valley or privacy risk?
4. How closely must avatar size and hand pose match the performer before true
   scale, pointing, and measurements can be trusted?
5. What motion-editing techniques can support segment retakes without forcing
   creators into a full animation workflow?

## Meaning

6. What research on common ground, conversational repair, social presence,
   distributed cognition, and embodied cognition should guide HoloGrammar?
7. How reliably can pointing and gaze be resolved to referents in real
   recordings, and what false-highlight rate do viewers tolerate?
8. Which symbolic primitives are learnable across domains without a training burden?

## View

9. When given a choice, how often do viewers stay in Follow versus switch to
   Free, on each device, and does either improve comprehension?
10. How can viewing analytics (where viewers stood, looked, replayed, and
    paused) measure comprehension while remaining privacy preserving?

## Conversation

11. What minimum feature set makes a remote AR participant feel co-present in a
    physical space?

## Additional Conversation Questions

12. Does an explanation with one manipulable object (IDEA-059), synchronized
   pause/viewpoint inspection (IDEA-060), and a capable flat-screen fallback
   improve a real follow-up task compared with a decent video explanation?
13. Which mixed-media layer (IDEA-061) or during-take authoring action (IDEA-062)
   adds meaning at a tolerable cost in speech interruption and scene clutter?
14. When are preview, discard/re-record, and trimming (IDEA-069) sufficient,
    and when do observed mistakes justify segment retakes or a layered editor?
15. Which creator pipeline failure first prevents repeat use: export/upload,
    previews, conversion, identity, or distribution
    (IDEA-046, IDEA-048 through IDEA-056, IDEA-058)?
16. Which audience and repeated job should lead: consumer expression, private
    team instruction/review, commerce explanation, or third-party integration
    (IDEA-064 through IDEA-067)?
17. How do we preserve voluntary shared attention, accurate spatial anchoring,
    and comfortable viewpoint changes across VR, AR, phone, and desktop?
18. What evidence distinguishes felt presence from visual realism or novelty,
    and how does consent or material editing affect that judgment (IDEA-063)?
19. What would make a result inconclusive rather than negative, and how will
    recipient availability, technical failure, learning/order effects, and
    author preparation effort affect the comparison?
