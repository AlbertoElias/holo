## Bicycle For The Mind As A Design Question

Treat the phrase as the user's aspiration for intellectual amplification, not
as evidence or a source attribution: can people express a thought they could
not readily express before, inspect competing interpretations, and improve
their understanding together? A fluent custom symbol, an inspectable example,
or a listener reconstruction might serve that purpose. Novel appearance alone
does not demonstrate it.

## Evidence Discipline

For each investigated source, record what the source studied, its population
and setting, the finding actually supported, how HoloIM differs, and a testable
product hypothesis. Preserve uncertainty around generalizing from laboratory
results, video calls, physical co-presence, or another device to holograms.
Use [open questions](open-questions.md) and the
[comparison method](../APPROACH.md) to turn inspiration into trials.
