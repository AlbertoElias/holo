# PRD: Counterfactual theater

**Status:** Parked  
**Source ideas:** IDEA-009  
**Layer:** Event  
**Depends on:** recorded object motion (PRD-043), a chosen decision task  
**Owner:** Alberto Elias  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-20

## Decision

Parked 2026-10-18 by the agreed lens in [what a hologram is](../concepts/what-a-hologram-is.md).
Rationale: simulated consequences are expensive, and no real decision task has
been chosen that justifies them; the registry's question ("which decisions
justify the cost of simulation?") is unanswered. Revisit when a team or design
trial (PRD-048) names a recurring decision between spatial alternatives that
people get wrong.

## User Outcome

Before: when two layouts or approaches are being compared, people imagine the
alternatives separately and argue past each other.
After: both alternatives play side by side in the same scene, A and B, with
their consequences shown, and the group decides with both in view.

## Problem

Comparing spatial alternatives in your head is hard; slides show them apart and
static.

## Primary User And Moment

A small group deciding between two arrangements of a room or two assembly
orders.

## Proposed Interaction

- The author records alternative A, then B, from the same start in the same scene.
- Viewers see them as two ghosts in one space, synchronized, and can switch
  which one is solid.
- Simulation of consequences (collision, reach, flow) only for one chosen task.

## Why Holograms

Two performances in one shared frame at true scale. Fair comparison: two videos
side by side.

- **Anchored to the event:** both alternatives in one scene.
- **Read from the body:** each alternative is performed.
- **Intent, not a view:** "compare these"; devices frame both.
- **Works on a flat phone:** both ghosts through the portal.
- **Lost as a video:** a shared frame both alternatives can be inspected in.

## Across Devices

| Device | Appearance | Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headset (VR or AR) | A and B as ghosts in the room | Switch solid |
| Phone portal | Same | Tap A or B |
| Desktop | Same | Click |
| Still image or Quick Look | Side-by-side stills | None |

## In Scope

- [ ] Two recorded alternatives in one scene, synchronized playback.

## Out Of Scope

- [ ] General simulation; more than two alternatives.

## Success Signals

- [ ] **Decision:** groups reach a decision faster with fewer misunderstandings.

## Risks And Open Questions

- [ ] Which decisions justify the cost of simulation?

## Comparison And Learning

If reopened: one real layout decision. Compare side-by-side holograms with two
videos. Measures: time to decision and whether participants describe the
difference the same way.
