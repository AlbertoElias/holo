# PRD: Team training and review, one team's trial

**Status:** Draft  
**Source ideas:** IDEA-065  
**Layer:** All layers  
**Depends on:** IDEA-059 or a real procedure to record; IDEA-052 if content must be private  
**Owner:** Unassigned  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-20

## User Outcome

Before: a team explains a physical procedure to newcomers with a document and
a video, and newcomers still need someone to show them in person.
After: we know whether one team, using holograms for one recurring procedure,
needs fewer in-person repeats and keeps using them without being asked.

## Problem

IDEA-065 is a market direction with libraries, annotations, and workspaces.
The first bounded outcome is one team, one repeated task. Workspaces and
access control only follow if the task proves useful.

## Primary User And Moment

A small team (a lab, a workshop, a makerspace) with a procedure taught to each
new member; an expert records, newcomers view on phones before doing it.

## Proposed Interaction

- The expert records the procedure in a Quest, with a supplied object where
  useful (PRD-043).
- Newcomers get a private link; they watch before their first attempt.
- The team keeps its current materials alongside.

## Why Holograms

The spatial-understanding promise. Fair comparison: the team's existing video
and document for the same procedure.

- **Anchored to the event:** the procedure in its real scale.
- **Read from the body:** pointing and hand paths (as available) carry steps.
- **Intent, not a view:** newcomers inspect from the side they need.
- **Works on a flat phone:** newcomers use their own phones.
- **Lost as a video:** viewpoints a fixed camera misses during the procedure.

## Across Devices

| Device | Appearance | Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headset (VR or AR) | Expert records; optional AR viewing beside equipment | Existing |
| Phone portal | Newcomers view | Portal |
| Desktop | Review by the expert | Mouse |
| Still image or Quick Look | Not needed | None |

## In Scope

- [ ] One team, one procedure, six weeks; observation in linked research.

## Out Of Scope

- [ ] Workspaces, roles, libraries, enterprise features.

## Success Signals

- [ ] **Task:** newcomers' first-attempt errors and requests for in-person
  demonstrations, versus the team's prior cohort or a parallel video group.
- [ ] **Adoption:** the expert records a second procedure unprompted.

## Risks And Open Questions

- [ ] Which team task benefits enough to adopt a shared spatial workflow?
- [ ] Small cohorts make comparison noisy; treat results as directional.

## Comparison And Learning

Continue toward team features if newcomers need fewer in-person demonstrations
and the team records more; park if the team falls back to video. Record
against research question 16.
