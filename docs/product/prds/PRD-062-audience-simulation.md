# PRD: Audience simulation

**Status:** Parked  
**Source ideas:** IDEA-030  
**Layer:** Meaning (read)  
**Depends on:** transcripts and resolved referents (PRD-022, PRD-009)  
**Owner:** Alberto Elias  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-20

## Decision

Parked 2026-10-18 by the agreed lens in [what a hologram is](../concepts/what-a-hologram-is.md).
Rationale: the [Approach](../APPROACH.md) states AI audience simulation can
suggest problems but cannot stand in for recipients, and the clarity
co-director (PRD-017) catches unresolved references with signals from the
event itself. Revisit if PRD-017 works and authors ask for feedback beyond
references; never as a substitute for recipient trials.

## User Outcome

Before: an author has no feedback before sending except their own judgement.
After: before sending, the author hears brief reactions from simulated
novice, expert, and skeptical viewers, and fixes the one point all three
found unclear.

## Problem

Authors cannot easily test a message before sending it.

## Primary User And Moment

An author at review, before publishing.

## Proposed Interaction

- A model reads the transcript, resolved referents, and marks, and produces
  short reactions from three perspectives, each tied to moments.
- Clearly labeled as simulated; never shown to recipients.

## Why Holograms

Fair comparison: no feedback, or a colleague's review.

- **Anchored to the event:** reactions point at moments and referents.
- **Read from the body:** works from what was read from the performance.
- **Intent, not a view:** suggestions only.
- **Works on a flat phone:** not shown to recipients.
- **Lost as a video:** reactions tied to spatial referents.

## Across Devices

| Device | Appearance | Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headset (VR or AR) | Reaction list in review | Jump to moment |
| Phone portal | Not shown | None |
| Desktop | Same list if review is on desktop | Click |
| Still image or Quick Look | Not applicable | None |

## In Scope

- [ ] Three simulated perspectives on one take, labeled as simulated.

## Out Of Scope

- [ ] Using simulation as evidence of comprehension.

## Success Signals

- [ ] **Agreement:** simulated issues overlap with issues real recipients raise.

## Risks And Open Questions

- [ ] Is AI simulation accurate enough to be trusted as feedback?

## Comparison And Learning

If reopened: compare simulated issues with real recipients' questions on the
same holograms. Continue only if overlap is high; park otherwise.
