# PRD: Consumer network, first repeat-use trial

**Status:** Draft  
**Source ideas:** IDEA-064  
**Layer:** All layers  
**Depends on:** IDEA-046 publish, IDEA-058 share link; IDEA-052 and IDEA-053 before any open sign-up  
**Owner:** Unassigned  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-20

## User Outcome

Before: we do not know whether anyone would make a second hologram for
friends, or come back to watch one, without being asked.
After: we know whether a small group of creators, given a frictionless way to
send holograms, keeps sending them for a month, and what they send.

## Problem

IDEA-064 is a market direction, not a feature. The README rule is one bounded
outcome per PRD, so this PRD scopes only the first test of the direction:
voluntary repeat creation and viewing. Growth loops, discovery, and moderation
at scale come only after repeat use exists.

## Primary User And Moment

Ten or so Quest owners who already share videos with friends; their friends
watching on phones.

## Proposed Interaction

- Creators get publish from capture (PRD-032) and share links (PRD-042).
- No feed ranking, follows, or likes; links are sent through their own chat apps.
- The team observes what they record and whether they return.

## Why Holograms

The direction rests on the human-presence promise. The fair comparison is the
videos these creators already send.

- **Anchored to the event / read from the body:** inherited from the messages.
- **Intent, not a view:** recipients view in the portal, not a fixed clip.
- **Works on a flat phone:** recipients are on phones.
- **Lost as a video:** if creators choose holograms only for things video
  cannot carry, that is the signal; if they record what a video would do, the
  direction is weak.

## Across Devices

| Device | Appearance | Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headset (VR or AR) | Capture with Publish | Existing |
| Phone portal | Share link | Portal |
| Desktop | Share link | Mouse |
| Still image or Quick Look | Link preview | None |

## In Scope

- [ ] A four-week trial with recruited creators; an observation log in linked research.

## Out Of Scope

- [ ] Feed algorithms, follows, notifications, monetization, open sign-up.

## Success Signals

- [ ] **Repeat use:** written before the trial; for example, half the creators
  send three or more unprompted holograms in weeks 3 and 4.
- [ ] **Recipients:** recipients reply or ask for more without prompting.

## Risks And Open Questions

- [ ] What recurring need would sustain creation and return visits?
- [ ] Novelty in week 1 is not evidence; look at weeks 3 and 4.
- [ ] Headset ownership limits the creator pool.

## Comparison And Learning

Compare with the video messages the same creators send in the same period
(self-reported). Continue toward a network if repeat use holds past novelty;
park if use stops after week 1. Record against research question 16.
